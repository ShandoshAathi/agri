# AgriSense AI - Platform User Manual

## Overview
AgriSense AI provides farm managers and farmers with live telemetry oversight, automated drip irrigation controls, and AI-driven agricultural recommendations.

---

## Key Modules & Navigation

### 1. Dashboard Overview (`/dashboard`)
- View total active acreage, live moisture levels, connected ESP32 nodes, and quick system status.

### 2. Sensor Telemetry (`/monitoring`)
- Real-time gauge cards displaying Volumetric Soil Moisture (%), Soil pH, Ambient Temperature (°C), Humidity (%), Rain Detection status, and Water Tank Level (%).

### 3. AI Crop Recommendation (`/recommendation`)
- Input soil pH, moisture, and rainfall parameters to receive AI recommended crop varieties, yield predictions, and farming advice.

### 4. Disease Diagnosis (`/diagnosis`)
- Upload leaf photographs to receive instant computer vision pathogen classification, severity ratings, and treatment protocols.

### 5. Smart Drip Irrigation (`/irrigation`)
- Toggle manual solenoid pump relay switches or configure automated moisture threshold rules with rain override safeguards.

### 6. Analytics & Exports (`/analytics`)
- Explore interactive Recharts visualizers for historical soil trends and export reports in PDF, CSV, or Excel formats.
