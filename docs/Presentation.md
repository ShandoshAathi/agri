# AgriSense AI - Project Presentation Outline

## Slide 1: Title & Executive Summary
- **Title**: AgriSense AI – AI & IoT Precision Farming Platform
- **Subtitle**: Optimizing Crop Yields with Real-Time Telemetry & Machine Learning

## Slide 2: Problem Statement
- Global water scarcity in agriculture & inefficient irrigation methods.
- Lack of real-time microclimate visibility leading to crop disease outbreaks.
- Non-optimal crop selection mismatched with local soil pH and moisture conditions.

## Slide 3: The Solution
- Unified IoT & AI platform connecting ESP32 microclimate nodes with Machine Learning advisors.
- Real-time automated drip irrigation control with rain sensor overrides.
- Computer Vision leaf pathogen scanner providing immediate treatment plans.

## Slide 4: System Architecture & Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, Recharts.
- **Backend**: FastAPI, Python 3.10, WebSockets.
- **AI/ML**: Scikit-learn (Crop Recommendation), TensorFlow/OpenCV (Disease Diagnosis).
- **IoT**: ESP32 C++ Firmware, MQTT (HiveMQ), Telemetry Simulator.

## Slide 5: Results & Impact
- Up to 28% water savings through precision drip automation.
- 18.5% average yield boost from AI-guided crop selection.
